FAQs

Find answers to your questions

Everything you need to know about our photography services and booking process.

What services do you offer?

We offer a variety of photography services including headshots, event photography, and editing classes. Our team is experienced in capturing moments from formal events to lively gatherings — whatever your photography needs, we've got you covered.

How do I book?

Booking a photographer is easy — reach out with your event details through the contact card below and we'll get back to you promptly to finalize arrangements.

What is your pricing?

Our pricing varies based on the type of service and duration of the event. We aim to provide competitive rates while ensuring high-quality results — see our pricing page for a full breakdown.

Do you offer editing?

Yes — we offer editing classes to help you learn how to enhance your photos. Our experienced photographers guide you through techniques and tools each semester.

Can I see examples?

Absolutely — check out our gallery for a range of our work, or follow along on Instagram for recent highlights.
